# Source attribution

This repository contains experiment scripts and results developed for a
transfer-validation study of:

- Microsoft Research Asia, **Ultra-Fast Neural Video Compression**, CVPR 2026;
- official source: <https://github.com/microsoft/DCVC>;
- dataset: **HySpecNet-11k**, TU Berlin RSiM.

The upstream DCVC source code, pretrained checkpoints, HySpecNet-11k data, and
other third-party assets are not redistributed in this repository. Fetch them
from their official sources and follow their respective licenses and terms.
